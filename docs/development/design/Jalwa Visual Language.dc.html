the rationale
jalwa means theophany — the dazzling manifestation of the divine as radiant light. So the app does exactly that: it takes the invisible (sound) and manifests it as light and form. The void is reverent emptiness; every glow is a note made visible. We keep the near-black-and-cyan soul the app already has, and let the GPU turn a quiet grid into something that feels, briefly, sacred.
